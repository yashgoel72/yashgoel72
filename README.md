## 🛠️ What I'm building

**[MeeShip](https://meeship.in)** is an AI platform for Indian e-commerce sellers on Meesho. It regenerates product photos so sellers pay less shipping, and it offers free tools for the rest of a seller's day (label crop, profit math, image resizing). Real users, real payments, and I run all of it: product, backend, frontend, growth and support.

**MeeShip Studio** ([meeship.in/studio](https://meeship.in/studio)) is a second product on the same engine. Sellers upload a product photo and get studio-quality scenes and marketplace-ready sizes, with credits, quality checks and automatic refunds when a job fails.

**[chandataare.com](https://chandataare.com)** is another site I run, with its own analytics and tracking.

## 🤖 Workflows I've automated with AI

| Area | What runs without me |
|---|---|
| **Content** | Daily content planning and drafting, with the best performers guiding the next batch |
| **Instagram** | Posting pipeline for Reels, carousels and Stories: scripts, hinglish voice-over, rendered video, upload, comment replies and a weekly self-review |
| **Video** | Automated Reel and short-video creation: script, voice, animation, captions |
| **Ops** | A daily log review of the backend that spots errors, failed logins and stuck jobs and turns them into fix proposals |
| **Analytics** | Google Analytics 4, Search Console and Microsoft Clarity pulled into regular reviews, so decisions come from data |
| **SEO and growth** | A weekly loop that finds what people search for, ships new guides and free tools, and checks what moved |

## 📈 Growth, at a high level

I treat growth as an engineering problem. I built an **inbound funnel** of free tools and guides that rank on search, feed the paid product, and are measured end to end in GA4. It grew traffic several times over without paid ads. I run A/B tests on UX changes, track funnels and fix what the logs and session recordings show.
